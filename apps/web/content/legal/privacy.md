# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]. Draft, not yet in effect._

This policy explains what personal data Quad collects, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your rights. **[COMPANY NAME]**, [COMPANY ADDRESS], is the controller of your data. Contact us at [PRIVACY EMAIL].

The short version:

- We collect what we need to verify you're an 18+ university student and to run your profile.
- We don't sell your data, and we don't use advertising trackers.
- Your date of birth, email and socials are never shown to other students. Socials are only shared with people you both choose to Connect with.
- You can delete your account yourself at any time.

## 1. What we collect

**Account and verification**

- Your **university email address**, and the university we recognize from it.
- Your **date of birth**. We use it only to check you're 18+. It's never shown to anyone.
- When you **verified** your email, and when that verification expires.
- If you're under 18, we delete the account and keep only a **one-way scrambled code** (a keyed hash) of your email so it can't sign up again. We can't turn this code back into your email.

**Profile**

- Display name, about text, course, year, interests, languages and card colour. Other students can see these on your Quad ID card.
- **Socials** (for example your Instagram handle), if you add them. These are private. Other students can only see them after you both press Connect.

**Signing in and security**

- **One-time codes.** We store them only in scrambled (hashed) form.
- **Session records.** For each device you're signed in on: a hashed session token, your IP address, your browser's user-agent string, and when the session was last used.
- **Short-lived counters** keyed by IP address or email, used to stop spam and brute-force attempts. These expire within 24 hours.

**Safety and moderation**

- Your account status (for example suspended or banned), the reason, and a log of actions our team takes.

**Things you choose to send us**

- **University requests.** If your university isn't recognized: your email, the university name, country and website you enter.
- **The Quad survey** (optional): which features you'd use, the hours you're usually free (converted to UTC) and your time zone.
- Anything you send when you contact support.

**On your device**

- One **cookie**, `quad_session`, which keeps you signed in. It's strictly necessary, so we don't ask for consent.
- Your **Calm mode** preference, saved in your browser's local storage.
- We don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Fonts are served from our own servers, not loaded from third parties.

## 3. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent personal data, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

We share data only with:

- **Service providers** that run Quad for us, under contracts that limit how they use it:
  - hosting ([HOSTING PROVIDER])
  - network and storage (Cloudflare: DNS, content delivery and encrypted backups)
  - email delivery (Resend)
- **Other students**, who can see your profile, as described above.
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety. For example, we report child sexual exploitation to the relevant authorities.
- **A buyer or successor**, if Quad is ever merged or sold. This policy would keep applying to your data.

## 4. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where the law requires it, we use safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 5. How long we keep it

| Data | How long |
|---|---|
| Account and profile | Until you delete your account |
| Sign-in codes | Deleted within a day after they expire (codes expire after 10 minutes) |
| Sessions (with IP address and user agent) | Deleted when they expire: 30 days after last use, or when you sign out |
| Sign-ups never finished | Deleted after 30 days |
| University requests | Deleted 180 days after we review them |
| Moderation log | 2 years |
| Banned accounts | The email, status and reason are kept while the ban is in force, so the ban can be enforced |
| Under-18 blocked-email hash | Kept indefinitely to prevent re-registration (it can't be turned back into your email) |
| Backups | Up to 14 days, then overwritten |

When you delete your account, your profile, socials, survey answers and sessions are deleted straight away. Copies in backups disappear within 14 days. If your account is under active investigation or banned, we may keep what's needed to enforce our Terms.

## 6. Your rights

- **withdraw consent** (for example, for the survey)

## 7. Age limit

Quad is only for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If we learn someone under 18 has signed up, we delete their account as described above. If you believe a minor is using Quad, report them in the app or email [PRIVACY EMAIL].

## 8. Security

- Sign-in codes, session tokens and blocked emails are stored hashed.
- All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
- Staff access to admin tools is limited and every action is logged.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.
