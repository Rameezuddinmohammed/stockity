01 · Principles

Six rules every screen follows

A PLACE

A lounge you walk into

Rooms, events and people online. There is no feed to scroll.

LOUD / QUIET

Loud edges, quiet middle

Lobbies are playful. During a call or a film, the UI steps back.

TACTILE

Everything is pressable

Ink outline and hard shadow; buttons sink into their shadow when tapped.

SAFETY

Calm, direct, one tap

Stickers and jokes switch off. Plain words, one clear action.

REAL

Real over perfect

No follower counts or filters. You collect countries, not likes.

GLOBAL

Every script, every time zone

Noto fallbacks, RTL layouts, and times shown in your time zone.

02 · Color

One bright color per mode

Bright colors are always backgrounds with ink text, never text on the page. An ink outline sits between any two brights, and red is only for safety.
